2Area

Moles are only looked for inside the area, and everything outside it is dimmed. A face photo uses the face without the eyes, brows, lips and nostrils. Other photos use the skin.

To fix it, drag over the photo with the Area tool. Add paints skin in and Erase takes it out.

3Scale

Drag the two marks onto the ruler with the Ruler tool, then type the distance between them.

Landmarks 1 and 2 set the scale.

4Landmarks

A face photo gets 6 landmarks on its own. Other segments are lined up on the moles themselves when a photo has no landmarks.

To add your own, tap fixed points with the Landmark tool, such as ink dots, the nipples or a scar, in the same order on both photos.

5Moles

Previous and Next zoom in on each mole in turn. Tap a missed mole with Add mole.
